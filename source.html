More than an auction

Build a team. Then prove it.

Footbauction is a live squad-building game for 2–10 friends. Everyone gets the same 100-credit budget; what you buy, where you play them and how you set up the team decide what happens next.

01

Win your players

Bid against your friends as goalkeepers, defenders, midfielders and forwards appear one by one.

02

Shape your XI

Choose a formation, put every player in the right role and select a Balanced, Counter or Aggressive tactic.

03

Watch it play out

The teams meet in a simulated league. Larger games add semifinals, a third-place match and the final.

Under the hood

Overall is only the headline

Matches are driven by individual player attributes—not one overall number. Passing, vision, finishing, pace, defending, physical ability and goalkeeper skills combine with position fit, formation and tactics. Stronger teams improve their odds, but never receive a guaranteed result.
